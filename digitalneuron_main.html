Data and interpretation

A scientifically cautious prototype

Region identities come from the 106-region DigitalBrain hierarchy. When linked to a Data Explorer selection, sampled regions are matched on the Brodmann axis and sized by real per-region cell counts.

Cell-type composition uses the Data Explorer's original taxonomy. Because the source data stores only marginal distributions (region totals and cell-type totals, not a joint region × cell-type matrix), the per-region composition shown is scope-level, not region-resolved.

Functional and structural connectivity are read from the project's 55-region FC and SC matrices; links are undirected and descriptively thresholded by matrix percentile.
