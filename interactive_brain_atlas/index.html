Data and interpretation

A scientifically cautious prototype

The region identities come from the 106-region DigitalBrain hierarchy used in the regional analysis. The broader atlas contains 165 harmonized region labels.

The built-in cell-class proportions are deterministic illustrative values, included so the interaction can be evaluated before the final abundance matrix is connected. They are not manuscript results.

Functional and structural connectivity are read from the project’s 55-region FC and SC matrices. Connectivity links are undirected, descriptively thresholded by matrix percentile and should not be interpreted as causal pathways.

Rainbow link hue encodes within-matrix connection-strength percentile. Region functions, DMN membership and connection explanations are curated orientation annotations; they are not inferred from the displayed matrix and are not subject-specific network assignments.

Region markers use fixed atlas-space anchors. Cell profiles are shown bilaterally; the non-lateralized FC/SC matrices use one fixed canonical-hemisphere anchor per region. Rotation changes only the viewpoint, never which nodes or links exist.

Anatomy comes from the bilateral 0.5-mm Allen Human Reference Atlas – 3D, 2020 annotation volume (141 voxel labels). The 106 analysis labels are hierarchical and overlapping. Forty-nine labels have exact or union-of-descendant geometry. Another 56 profiles are displayed on curated coarse or gyral proxies so cortex, hippocampus and brainstem remain visible; their details explicitly identify that lower mapping precision. The spinal-cord label is outside this brain volume.
